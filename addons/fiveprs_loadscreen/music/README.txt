Loading screen music

Put .mp3 or .ogg files in this folder, then list them in config.js:

    music: {
        tracks: ["music/patrol.mp3", "music/night-shift.ogg"],
        volume: 0.3,
        shuffle: true,
    },

Tracks can also be direct links to audio files, e.g. "https://example.com/theme.mp3".
Streaming pages such as YouTube or Spotify are not supported.

Only use music you have the rights to distribute with your server.
Players can mute it with the button in the top-right corner; their choice is remembered.
